About me

Hi, I'm Kamali G from Dindigul, currently pursuing my final year in Information Technology. I am passionate about Frontend Development and enjoy creating modern, responsive, and user-friendly web applications. My interest in web development started with learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I have continuously improved my skills by working with Bootstrap, React, Git, and GitHub through hands-on practice and personal projects. One of my favorite projects is developing my own portfolio website, where I apply responsive design principles and create clean, user-friendly interfaces. I have also participated in the Zoho Hackathon, where my team developed a voice-to-text email assistant for Zoho Mail, and the TN Impact Hackathon, where we built a Voice AI Logistics Assistant for delivery personnel. Our solution was selected as a winning project, which gave me valuable experience in teamwork, problem-solving, and building real-world applications. I enjoy learning new technologies, exploring modern frontend frameworks, and continuously improving my coding skills by working on practical projects. My goal is to become a skilled Frontend Developer and work in a reputed company where I can contribute to innovative products, learn from experienced professionals, and grow both personally and professionally while building impactful digital experiences.
